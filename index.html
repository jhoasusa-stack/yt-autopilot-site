yt-autopilot

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel “El Precio de No Saber”.

What it is

yt-autopilot is a command-line program that runs on its owner’s personal computer. It helps one creator, the owner of the channel, produce educational videos in Spanish about personal finance and publish them to YouTube channels that he owns. Every video is reviewed and approved by him before it is published.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads the owner’s videos to the owner’s channels and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics for the owner’s own channels to decide future topics.

It is a single-user tool. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not collect data about anyone else.
